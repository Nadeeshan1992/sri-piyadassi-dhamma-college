“Guiding Tomorrow’s Generation Through the Dhamma”

Sri Piyadassi Dhamma College is dedicated to nurturing morally upright, compassionate, and wise young generations through the teachings of the Buddha. Our mission is to impart Buddhist values, develop good character, promote spiritual growth, and inspire children to live with kindness, mindfulness, discipline, and respect for all living beings.

We provide a supportive learning environment where students can deepen their understanding of Buddhism, strengthen their moral values, discover their talents, and become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

Academic Structure

Dhamma Education & Classes

☸

A comprehensive, step-by-step Buddhist educational framework conforming to the Department of Buddhist Affairs syllabus.

Grades 1 to 5 • Ages 5 – 10

Foundations of Buddhist Culture & Morals

Introducing young minds to the compassionate life of the Buddha, simple gathas, the Five Precepts, loving-kindness meditation, and inspiring Jataka tales that nurture respect for parents, teachers, and all creatures.

  • Buddha Charithaya (Life of the Buddha)
  • Gatha Vandana & Pahan Puja
  • Jataka Tales & Moral Ethics
  • Sinhala & Buddhist Traditions
  • Daily Mindfulness & Good Habits
  • Creative Dhamma Art & Chanting
Grades 6 to 9 • Ages 11 – 14

Intermediate Dhamma Studies & Buddhist History

Deepening analytical understanding of the Four Noble Truths, the Noble Eightfold Path, the spread of Buddhism in Sri Lanka, and foundational Pali language skills.

  • The Four Noble Truths & Eightfold Path
  • History of Sasana in Sri Lanka
  • Introduction to Elementary Pali
  • Buddhist Social Philosophy
  • Dhammapada Stanzas & Commentary
  • Basic Abhidhamma Analysis
Grades 10 & 11 • Ages 15 – 17

Daham Pasala Final Examination Preparation

Rigorous preparation for the National Dhamma School Final Certificate Examination (දහම් පාසල් අවසාන සහතික පත්‍ර විභාගය) with thorough coverage of Buddhist philosophy, culture, and ethics.

  • Advanced Buddhist Culture
  • Buddhist Philosophy & Sutta Studies
  • Intermediate Pali Grammar & Texts
  • Past Examination Paper Workshops
  • Leadership & Dhamma Dissemination
  • Oratory & Debating Skills
Higher Level • Ages 17+ & Adults

Dharmacharya Diploma & Teacher Training

The prestigious national qualification for prospective Dhamma school instructors, exploring deep canonical scriptures, Pali Canon literature, comparative religion, and pedagogy.

  • Advanced Abhidhamma Pitaka
  • Sutta Pitaka & Vinaya Pitaka
  • Pali Language & Literature
  • Buddhist Civilization & Heritage
  • Pedagogy & Teaching Methodologies
  • Community Spiritual Leadership
